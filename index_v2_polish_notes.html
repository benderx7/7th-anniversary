One Last Page

Before We Close This Chapter...

I know... this website reached you a little earlier than our anniversary. Honestly... I wasn't sure if we'd still be together on the actual day. So I didn't want to miss the chance to give this to you.

Thank you... For every smile. Every late-night call. Every risky car meet. Every prayer. Every hug. Every little moment that became a memory. I'll always be grateful for all of it.

I really wished I could add more memories here. More photos. More moments. More pages. Unfortunately... Life had different plans. Maybe that's why this website isn't filled with hundreds of memories. Instead... It's filled with my heart.

If you ever have the time... Please go through everything once. Look at our photo. Listen to every voice note. They're all a part of me that I wanted to leave with you.

I genuinely hope your future is beautiful. I hope you always stay happy. I hope life gives you everything you've ever wished for. You deserve peace. You deserve love. You deserve happiness.

And one last thing... Whether I'm a part of your life or not... I'll always be one of the people quietly wishing the best for you. Not because I have to. But because I genuinely want to. If life ever brings you back someday... You'll always find kindness from my side.

Take care of yourself. Always. 🤍
